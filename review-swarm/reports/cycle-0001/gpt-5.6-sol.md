### The occupancy ladder relabels prompt accessibility as parameter storage

- **Severity:** S0
- **Target:** `ops/crowding/occupancy.py:88,107-165`; `evals/storage.py:1-7`; `ops/crowding/crowding_ladder.sh:15-20`; `docs/PAPER-MEASUREMENT.md:374-417`
- **Claim under attack:** The occupancy ladder “tests premise 1 directly,” so a plateau in `recoverable_bits_per_param` means capacity binds, a fall means abandonment, and the held-out probe is a “direct observation” that the model declined to store the mappings.
- **Defect:** `evals/storage.py` explicitly says its prompt-conditioned NLL cannot separate what is stored from what one prompt can induce the model to emit and is therefore deliberately not called storage. `occupancy.py` nevertheless reads `recoverable_bits_per_param` into `stored_bits_per_param` and assigns capacity-mechanism verdicts to its shape. A zero trained-minus-unseen difference under one held-out phrasing is also compatible with mappings that are present but inaccessible under that phrasing. The new `probe_control.py` acknowledges exactly this by requiring a training-phrasing positive control, but that control has not been run in any retained artifact. The long paper reaches the abandonment conclusion before the instrument has passed that control.
- **Why existing checks miss it:** `tests/test_occupancy.py` writes synthetic values under the key `recoverable_bits_per_param` and then asserts that the renamed “storage” signatures fire, so it codifies the substitution rather than challenging it. `tests/test_storage.py` checks accounting and uses an oracle stub that reads the future target from the teacher-forced tensor; it does not calibrate the held-out prompt on a checkpoint known to contain mappings. `tests/test_probe_control.py` hard-codes hypothetical gaps and never runs the reported checkpoint.

Do not interpret any occupancy signature until this reports the trained-minus-unseen gap under both training and held-out phrasing and the same held-out probe has separated trained from unseen entities on a known-positive checkpoint.
- **What would prove me wrong:** A known-positive memorizing checkpoint separates trained from unseen entities under these held-out templates, and the final checkpoint shows no separation under either the training phrasing or several held-out phrasings, with an independent parameter-storage measurement agreeing.

### Gate 0 averages microbatch means instead of fact-value tokens

- **Severity:** S1
- **Target:** `train/trainer.py:147-167`; `train/model.py:232-235`; `docs/RETAINED-RESULTS.md:338-345`; `docs/PAPER-MEASUREMENT.md:363-366`
- **Claim under attack:** Gate 0 is the mean cross-entropy “at fact-value token positions,” reported as 1.9732 nats.
- **Defect:** Each call to `self.model(xb, yb)` uses default mean reduction over the surviving masked-value targets in that microbatch. `loss_masked_values` then gives every microbatch mean equal weight, regardless of how many fact-value targets it contains. The result is a mean of microbatch means, not the per-token mean named in the paper. An in-memory execution with two chunks containing 1 and 8 live targets returned 4.133627 from `loss_masked_values`, exactly the equal-chunk mean, while one forward pass over all nine targets returned 4.150990. The qualitative claim that 1.97 is far below 25 may survive, but the published quantity has not been computed as stated.
- **Why existing checks miss it:** `tests/test_weighted_loss.py` thoroughly pins the fixed denominator for training, but Gate 0 deliberately uses the unweighted model path. `test_loss_decreases_and_logs` checks only that a `loss_masked_values` field exists, and `test_masked_value_probe` checks only that some labels survive. No test varies target count across probe microbatches and compares against a global token sum.
- **Falsifiable check:** Add a regression test with two probe chunks containing unequal numbers of non-`-100` labels and assert `Trainer.loss_masked_values()` equals `sum(per_token_ce) / count_live_targets`, not the arithmetic mean of chunk losses. On the production probe, print each chunk’s live-target count and recompute 1.9732 from one global numerator and denominator.
- **What would prove me wrong:** Every production probe chunk has exactly the same live-target count, or the globally token-weighted recomputation agrees with 1.9732 at the reported precision.
