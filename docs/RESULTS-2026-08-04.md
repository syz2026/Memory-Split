# Results, 2026-08-04 — the endpoint was never the problem

Internal note. Supersedes the endpoint conclusions in
`docs/THEORY-ENDPOINT.md` and the STOP in `outputs/pilot/stage_a_report.json`.

## Summary

A padding defect in batched greedy decoding made every generative accuracy
number this project has produced read at floor. With it fixed, the same
checkpoint that scored **4.7%** scores **93.8%**. Four experiment generations
concluded the reasoning endpoint was unusable at accessible scale. It is not,
and never was.

## The defect

`evals/generate.py::generate_batch` left-padded every prompt to the batch
maximum with EOT and ran attention over the pads. There was no attention mask.
EOT is the document separator, so a prompt sitting behind enough pad tokens
reads as the start of a fresh document and the model invents its premises
rather than reading the ones it was given.

Measured on `stepladder_d40m_std` at step 31,280, 64 held-out iGSM items at
MOD 23:

| decoding | accuracy |
|---|---:|
| batched, padded to the batch maximum — as every eval in this project ran | **3/64 = 4.7%** |
| one prompt at a time | **60/64 = 93.8%** |
| batched, after the fix | **60/64 = 93.8%** |

Corruption is monotone in pad length: exact at 0–16 pad tokens, broken by 32.
Because padding is the gap between a prompt and the longest prompt in its
batch, the damage fell hardest on the shortest prompts.

**That explains the two anomalies previously attributed to the model.** Mean
prompt length rises with operation count — 100, 136, 206 and 245 tokens for
op 1 to 4 — so short problems were padded most and scored worst. The
"backwards" op profile, where accuracy rose with difficulty, was a padding
artifact, not the constant-answer signature it was read as. And the 47% of
generations that fell outside the answer space, diagnosed on 2026-08-03 as a
formatting failure, were corrupted prompts: the first snapshot scored after the
fix reports an in-answer-space rate of **0.9993**.

The fix decodes in groups of exactly equal prompt length, so no padding exists
and no mask is required. `tests/test_generate.py` asserts that batching never
changes a generation.

The storage probe is unaffected: `evals/storage.py` pads on the **right**, and
under causal attention a token never attends to positions after it.

## The endpoint, measured properly for the first time

Same checkpoint, 48 items per band:

| op | accuracy | |
|---:|---:|---|
| 1 | 10/10 = 100% | ceiling |
| 2 | 17/17 = 100% | ceiling |
| 3 | 9/9 = 100% | ceiling |
| 4 | 9/12 = 75% | **responsive** |
| 5 | 5/11 = 45% | **responsive** |
| 6 | 0/12 = 0% | floor |
| 7 | 0/17 = 0% | floor |
| 8 | 1/8 = 12% | floor |

The model solves three-operation dependency chains perfectly and falls off a
cliff at six. In-band (op 1–4) aggregates to 93.8%, which fails
`capacity.responsive()` from the ceiling side. Out-of-band (op 5–8) is 12.5%,
carried entirely by op 5. Note op 5–8 was never trained on: the corpus was
built with `op_band [1,4]`, so those columns measure length generalisation.

A partial learning curve is already available from the snapshot scoring: at
step 1,564 — 5% of training — accuracy is **19.6%**, against 93.8% at step
31,280.

## What this overturns

Everything downstream of a generated answer:

- Stage A's STOP, and its rationale that "the endpoint has no power to
  discriminate anything".
- Both difficulty ladders, including the 2026-08-02 rebuild on valid corpora
  and its verdict that the task is "NOT LEARNABLE even at mod 5".
- `docs/THEORY-ENDPOINT.md` in full — the step-budget argument, the comparison
  to the grokking literature, and the claim that 1,525 steps was 7x short. The
  endpoint was never step-limited.
- The 1B held-out result, the 0.8B gate evaluations, and every per-op and
  per-class breakdown in `docs/RETAINED-RESULTS.md` §1.
- The scale-window and NO-GO framing, which rested on the endpoint being
  unusable at accessible scale.

## What survives

Anything that never passed through generation:

- The exposure census: 1.04–1.55 exposures per fact on the reasoning-v3 corpus.
- Recoverable bits, and therefore every storage measurement.
- The loss-based arm contrasts in the tiny cohort (+0.0009 to +0.0028 nats).
- The loss-normalization confound.
- The RANDPOS difficulty gap: 1.9598 nats against 1.2712, a 35.1% relative gap
  against a 20% tolerance, structural and unfixable at any placement tolerance.
- The lane-share substitution that invalidated Stage A's corpus.
- Gate 0 on this run: **1.9732 nats** at fact-value positions, against 25.03 in
  the 2026-08-02 diagnosis. The facts were genuinely stored this time.

## Consequences

The programme's central obstacle is removed and replaced by a smaller one. The
endpoint is not at floor; it is at ceiling in-band, with a narrow responsive
window at op 4–5. A corpus with `op_band [1,8]` is building now
(job 1675799, CPU, `normal` partition) with the fact configuration unchanged at
996,408 entities x 200 exposures, F/C = 1.000, so the crowding operating point
is preserved and only the reasoning difficulty widens. The op band is an
unfilled `[PILOT]` slot in `docs/PREREGISTRATION.md` §5, so freezing it from a
SUP-only difficulty curve is the preregistered procedure rather than a
post-hoc choice.

**The conclusion that Memory Split is false in the no-crowding regime needs
re-examination.** Its mechanism argument — that nothing was stored, so nothing
could be freed — rests on the exposure census and the storage probe, and both
survive. Its reasoning-accuracy tables do not.
